Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Nearon: Bluetooth Finder ("Nearon", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Nearon collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our application.

Overview

Nearon is a Bluetooth device discovery and tracking application that helps users locate nearby Bluetooth-enabled devices using signal strength, directional indicators, saved devices, and optional map-based features.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and minimizing data collection wherever possible.

Information We Collect

Nearon does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or payment information.

Most application functionality operates entirely on your device without transmitting personal information to external servers.

Bluetooth Data

Nearon requires Bluetooth access to discover nearby Bluetooth devices and provide tracking functionality.

The application may display information broadcast by nearby Bluetooth devices including:

  • Device names
  • Bluetooth identifiers
  • Signal strength (RSSI)
  • Advertisement information
  • Device type information

This information is processed locally on your device.

Location Information

Apple requires location permissions for Bluetooth scanning functionality.

Nearon may use your location to:

  • Enable Bluetooth device discovery
  • Support map-based device projections
  • Provide optional location-related features
  • Improve saved device tracking functionality

Location information is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

Saved Devices

When you save a device within Nearon, information such as device names, signal strength, estimated distance, and optional location information may be stored locally on your device.

Saved device information remains under your control and may be removed at any time through the application.

Subscriptions

Nearon offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions through Apple's App Store.

Subscription purchases, renewals, cancellations, and billing information are processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment information.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share personal information with third parties.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information stored locally on your device. However, no security system can guarantee absolute protection.

Children's Privacy

Nearon is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-Party Services

Nearon may utilize Apple services required for application functionality, subscriptions, and App Store distribution.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
